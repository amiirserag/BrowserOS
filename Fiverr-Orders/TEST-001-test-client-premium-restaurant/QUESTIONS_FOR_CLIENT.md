# Questions for the client

These block a real-world listing. The preview uses labeled sample copy until they are answered.

1. What is the restaurant’s public name, and is there a legal name that must appear in the footer?
2. Please send the logo in SVG or high-resolution PNG. Do not ask us to redraw it.
3. Please send dining-room, food, and team photography you own, or confirm we should keep CSS-only decoration.
4. Send the live menu: categories, dishes, descriptions, prices, and currency.
5. Street address, city, and any map embed you want used.
6. Public phone, reservations email, and optional WhatsApp or booking link (OpenTable / Resy / custom).
7. Opening hours, last seating, and holiday closures.
8. Reservation rules (party size, deposit, dress code) to print on Contact.
9. Social profile URLs, if any.
10. Confirm or reject the dark cinematic direction. If you have brand colors, send hex values.
